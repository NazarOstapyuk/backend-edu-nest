# Cash desk Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A «Каса» module: cash desks with shifts, cash child payments landing in the open shift, expenses and confirmed transfers, recount by denominations, closing with explanation, corrections, in-CRM alerts for the manager, cash report with Excel.

**Architecture:** Immutable ledger (`CashEntry`) per shift; the expected balance of an open shift is computed from `openingCounted` and non-voided entries, a closed shift stores a snapshot. Pure money logic in `src/cash/cash-calc.ts`; services in `src/cash`; child payments create the `income` entry in the same transaction. Frontend page `pages/cash`, payment form fields, dashboard rows, reports tab.

**Tech Stack:** Backend NestJS 11, Prisma 7 (PostgreSQL), Jest. Frontend React 19, MUI 9, Zustand, react-hook-form + yup.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-cash-desk-design.md` (backend repo). Read it first.

Repos: backend `D:\IT\backend-edu-nest` (tasks B1-B7), frontend `D:\IT\EduNest` (tasks F1-F6). Backend tasks first.

## Review Focus

1. Two operators acting at once (two expenses together, or closing while an expense is recorded) must not drive the balance below zero or create two open shifts: all desk mutations lock the desk row (`SELECT id FROM "CashDesk" WHERE id = $1 FOR UPDATE`) inside the transaction. Test in B3/B4 that the lock query runs before the balance read.
2. Voiding a cash payment whose shift is already closed must not change the closed shift: it creates a `correction` (`sign -1`) in the open shift; without an open shift the payment void is refused. Test in B5.
3. A difference of exactly 0.00 must not require an explanation; 0.10 must. Test in B2/B3.
4. An income entry cannot be voided directly (only through its payment), and nothing in a closed shift can be voided. Test in B4.
5. A transfer recipient who is the creator, a parent, or inactive is refused; only the recipient can confirm or dispute, and only once. Test in B4.
